About the project

Food waste management starts with coordination.

FoodRescue is a simple academic project exploring how a clear donation workflow can help usable surplus food reach people who need it.

Our aim

Reduce avoidable food waste and encourage responsible donation through an accessible, easy-to-understand platform.

What we do

We collect donation details, provide pickup information, and make the progress of each record visible to its donor.

Our activities

The demo supports account creation, donation records, status updates, and basic administration using browser storage.

Practical habits

How food waste can be reduced

Plan portions

Buy and prepare amounts that match the needs of the household or event.

Share safely

Identify safe surplus food early and provide clear information about its contents.

Track outcomes

Use simple records to understand what was donated and where the process can improve.

Collaborators and contributions

In a real implementation, contributors could include donors, volunteers, community groups, restaurants, and organizations working on food access. This student project does not claim partnerships with any real organization.

Everyone can contribute by donating safe surplus food, sharing accurate pickup details, or helping others learn about responsible food use.
